# Sound wishlist

What the dungeon still plays a shared, generic sound for (audited 2026-11-18, v3.37.185).
382 abilities share 46 sound files. Two files carry almost half of them:

- `spell_buff_invoke.mp3` — 115 abilities (every buff, ward, summon and utility spell without its own sound)
- `spell_umbral_bolt.mp3` — 67 abilities (every dark, death and curse spell)

One new sound per line below would give each family its own voice. Ordered by how often Josh hears them.
Drop files in `public/audio/` and name them anything; tell me which line each one is for.

## Buffs and wards (all on the generic invoke sound today)

1. **Mage Armor** — a soft force shimmer settling on the body.
2. **Shield** — a hard, short force "clang" snapping into place.
3. **Stoneskin / Stoneskin (Communal) / Iron Skin / Barkskin** — stone or bark grinding over skin.
4. **Protection from Energy (Communal), all five / Resist Energy, all five** — an elemental hum; one file is fine.
5. **Animal buffs: Bull's Strength, Bear's Endurance, Cat's Grace, Eagle's Splendor, Fox's Cunning, Owl's Wisdom
   and their Mass versions** — a short animal call or a rising tone; one file is fine.
6. **Heroism / Greater Heroism / Aid / Rage (the spell)** — a brass or drum swell.
7. **Greater Magic Weapon (Communal) / Magic Weapon / Keen Edge / Bless Weapon / Magic Vestment / Magic Fang** — a
   blade being honed, a ringing edge.
8. **Spell Turning / Spell Resistance / Globe of Invulnerability (both) / Antilife Shell / Mind Blank** — a glassy
   ward or bubble forming.
9. **Enlarge Person / Mass Enlarge Person / Transformation / Elemental Body** — a stretching, growing sound.
10. **True Seeing / See Invisibility / Darkvision (Communal) / Foresight** — a bright "eye opening" chime.
11. **The aligned auras: Holy Aura, Unholy Aura, Cloak of Chaos, Shield of Law / Protection from Evil (Communal) /
    Death Ward / Sanctuary** — a choir pad (one holy, optionally one unholy).
12. **Fire Shield** — a whoosh of flame wrapping the body.
13. **Air Walk / Freedom of Movement** — a light wind lift.

## Summons (all on the generic invoke sound today)

14. **Summon Monster I-IX** — a portal tear or planar pop.
15. **Summon Nature's Ally I-IX** — a horn or animal call from the wild.
16. **Summon Devil I-VII** — a brimstone gate (Jason's game).
17. **Summon Undead I-IX / Animate Dead** — bones assembling, a grave opening (Draymus's game; on the dark sound today).

## Movement, time and walls

18. **Dimension Door / Teleport** — a short "blink" pop out and in.
19. **Time Stop** — a clock tick winding down to silence.
20. **Wish / Limited Wish / Miracle** — something grand.
21. **Wall of Force / Forcecage / Resilient Sphere / Wind Wall** — a wall slamming up.
22. **Wall of Thorns / Spike Stones** — cracking wood or stone spikes.
23. **Maze** — a door echoing shut in a large space.
24. **Solid Fog / Fog Cloud / Obscuring Mist** — a hiss of rolling fog.

## Dark and death magic (all on the umbral bolt today)

25. **Death spells: Finger of Death, Slay Living, Destruction, Wail of the Banshee, Phantasmal Killer, Weird,
    Implosion, Circle of Death, Power Word Kill** — a death knell.
26. **Inflict Wounds (all eight) / Harm / Chill Touch / Vampiric Touch / Touch of Corruption** — a cold, wet drain.
27. **Energy Drain / Enervation / Waves of Fatigue / Waves of Exhaustion / Ray of Exhaustion / Ray of
    Enfeeblement** — a deflating, life-leaving sound.
28. **Curses: Bestow Curse, Doom, Bane, Crushing Despair, Eyebite** — a whispered hex.
29. **Mind-breakers: Confusion, Lesser Confusion, Insanity, Feeblemind** — a warped, detuned chord.
30. **Blindness / Power Word Blind / Power Word Stun** — a sharp spoken-word crack.
31. **Darkness / Deeper Darkness** — light being swallowed.
32. **Blasphemy / Word of Chaos / Unholy Blight / Chaos Hammer** — an unholy word (the holy ones have theirs).
33. **Web** — strands snapping taut.

## Class moves with no sound of their own

34. **Druid forms: Tiger, Bear, Hawk, Lion, Dire Ape, Dire Boar, Crocodile / Beast Mode / Promethean** — a growl or
    shape-shift per form, or one shared shift sound.
35. **Maneuvers: Grapple, Trip, Bull Rush, Disarm, Feint** — a body hitting the floor, a scuffle, a weapon skittering away.
36. **Enemy sidearms (new in v3.37.185): thrown javelin, sling, thrown dagger, giant's boulder** — they use the bow
    shot and the blunt-weapon hit for now.
37. **Gunslinger deeds: Dead Shot, Up Close and Deadly** — a slowed-down heavy shot.
